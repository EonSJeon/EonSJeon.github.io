## Overview

Time-interleaved analog-to-digital converters enable high-speed sampling by combining multiple lower-rate converters. Differences in their gain, sampling timing, and offset can introduce distortion in OFDM reception.

This research studies how to estimate these hardware mismatches alongside the wireless channel from known pilot signals. The work focuses on practical estimation challenges and computationally efficient receiver-side signal processing.

## Research status

The research project was completed over **September 2024–October 2026**. A related manuscript is in preparation and has not yet been submitted.
