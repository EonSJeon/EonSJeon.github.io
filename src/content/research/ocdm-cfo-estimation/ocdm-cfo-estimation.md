## Overview

Orthogonal chirp division multiplexing (OCDM) is a multicarrier waveform implemented through the discrete Fresnel transform. Carrier frequency mismatch can degrade synchronization and signal detection, especially when the offset extends beyond a narrow local range.

This project investigates wide-range carrier frequency offset estimation using a single pilot block in multipath channels. It combines estimation-theoretic modeling, CFO identifiability analysis, and simulation-based evaluation to study synchronization in chirp-based communication systems.

## Research focus

The work examines how to reason about frequency acquisition range and develop model-based estimators for frequency-selective multicarrier waveforms. The aim is a principled framework for reliable synchronization with a compact pilot structure.

A manuscript describing this research has been submitted to **ICASSP 2027** and is currently under review.
